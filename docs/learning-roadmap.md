# Cold Chain Supply Chain Management — Learning Roadmap

## Project Objective

Learn cold chain management and SQL together through structured study, practical exercises, database design, and business analysis.

## Stage 1 — Fundamentals

**Cold Chain Topics**
- Understand cold chain supply chain management.
- Learn chilled, frozen, and temperature-controlled logistics.
- Understand facilities, products, inventory lots, and shipments.
- Map the end-to-end cold chain process.

**SQL Topics**
- Databases, tables, rows, and columns.
- Primary keys and foreign keys.
- SELECT, WHERE, ORDER BY, and LIMIT.

**Learning Output**
- Explain the cold chain process.
- Write basic SQL queries.
- Describe the purpose of each database table.

## Stage 2 — Data Relationships

**Cold Chain Topics**
- Inventory traceability.
- Storage locations.
- Shipment contents and temperature history.

**SQL Topics**
- INNER JOIN and LEFT JOIN.
- Relationships between tables.
- NULL values and missing records.

**Learning Output**
- Connect products, inventory lots, facilities, and shipments.

## Stage 3 — Operational Analysis

**Cold Chain Topics**
- Inventory ageing and expiry.
- Temperature excursions.
- Shipment performance and operational KPIs.

**SQL Topics**
- COUNT, SUM, AVG, MIN, and MAX.
- GROUP BY and HAVING.
- CASE WHEN and date filtering.

**Learning Output**
- Analyze temperature readings.
- Identify inventory approaching expiry.
- Summarize shipment status and incidents.

## Stage 4 — Data Quality and Reliability

**Cold Chain Topics**
- Traceability gaps.
- Temperature monitoring.
- Incident documentation and corrective actions.

**SQL Topics**
- NOT NULL, UNIQUE, CHECK, and foreign keys.
- Data-quality checks.
- Views and Common Table Expressions (CTEs).

**Learning Output**
- Identify missing or inconsistent data.
- Explain how database constraints protect data quality.

## Stage 5 — Business Economics

**Cold Chain Topics**
- CAPEX and OPEX.
- Storage and transportation costs.
- Capacity utilization and spoilage risk.
- Pricing and unit economics.

**SQL Topics**
- Multi-table aggregation.
- Window functions.
- Analytical queries and reusable views.

**Learning Output**
- Define operational KPIs.
- Analyze cost drivers and business performance.
- Document assumptions and limitations.

## Stage 6 — Integrated Capstone

- Design a cold chain business case.
- Build and validate a relational database.
- Analyze operational performance.
- Develop a financial model.
- Document risks and recommendations.
- Present evidence-based business conclusions.

## Definition of Completion

A topic is complete when I can:
1. Explain the concept in my own words.
2. Apply it to a practical example.
3. Write or interpret the relevant SQL.
4. Validate the results.
5. Explain the business implications and limitations.

**Learning principle:** Understand → Model → Query → Analyze → Validate → Improve.
